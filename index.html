About

Fifteen-plus years shipping software inside a globally distributed organization.

I'm a full-stack engineer who has spent my career building the enterprise web applications that keep a 70-plus-school institution running — student data systems, scheduling tools, and staff workflow platforms used daily across three continents. My core stack is the Microsoft ecosystem: C#, VB.NET, and ASP.NET on SQL Server, extended with SharePoint and the Power Platform, and I'm currently leading a migration of legacy Web Forms systems to modern .NET 8 with Clean Architecture.

More recently I've been integrating large language models into production tooling — retrieval-augmented assistants, automated document analysis, and AI-assisted workflows that genuinely save people time. I completed my M.S. in Software Engineering in 2024 and I'm relocating to the Puget Sound region, where I own property on Camano Island, to take on my next role.
